## Signing

To sign this CLA:

1. **Fork** the CorvinOS repository
2. **Create** your plugin in `operator/marketplace/plugins/buildin/[category]/[plugin_id]/`
3. **Include** this signed statement in your pull request:

   ```
   I, [Full Name], hereby sign the Buildin Plugin CLA dated 2026-08-31.
   I agree to the terms and conditions outlined above.
   
   Signed: [Full Name]
   Email: [Your Email]
   Date: [Date]
   ```

4. **Submit** your PR; Anthropic will verify your CLA signature
5. **Merge:** Once verified, your plugin becomes a buildin contribution
